# Build a website with AI — this week

![Scan to start](qr-start.png)

**Scan to start:** https://student-sites.org/start
**Class passcode:** `greatestclass`

## Steps

1. **Pair up and pick a site name.** Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens only — e.g. `sams-recipes`. This becomes your web address: `student-sites.org/students/sams-recipes/`. Use the same name every time you submit.
2. **Scan the QR code** and download the starter kit. Unzip it and rename the `my-site` folder to your site name.
3. **Build it with AI.**
   - *Claude Code:* open a terminal in your folder, run `claude`, describe the site you want. It reads the rules in `CLAUDE.md` automatically.
   - *ChatGPT:* paste the contents of `PROMPT.md` into the chat, then describe your site. Save each file it gives you into your folder.
4. **Check it.** Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser. Keep iterating until you're happy.
5. **Fill in `meta.json`** — title, one-sentence description, a few tags. This is what shows on the index page.
6. **Zip and submit.** Right-click your folder → *Compress* (Mac) or *Send to → Compressed folder* (Windows). Go to **student-sites.org/submit**, enter your site name, first names, the passcode, and upload the zip.
7. **Keep your review link.** The submit page gives you a link showing whether your site is waiting, published, or needs changes. Your teacher approves each version before it goes live. Resubmit as often as you like — the newest upload replaces the old one.

## Rules

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Everything inside your one folder, under 10 MB.
- Classroom-appropriate. First names only — no surnames, contact details or student IDs.
- Only use images you're allowed to use.
- Every submission is checked by your teacher before it's published.
