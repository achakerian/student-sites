What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks which normally require human intelligence, such as recognizing images, understanding language, making decisions, and learning from experience. Instead of following one fixed set of instructions, an AI system can improve its behavior by learning patterns from data.

Types of AI

Machine Learning

Algorithms that find patterns in data and improve automatically, like a spam filter that gets smarter the more email it sees.

Natural Language Processing

Helps computers understand and generate human language, used in chatbots, translation, and voice assistants.

Computer Vision

Lets machines interpret images and video, powering things like face unlock and self-driving car cameras.

Robotics

Combines AI with physical machines so robots can sense their environment and act on it, from vacuum robots to factory arms.

A Short History of AI

1950

Alan Turing proposes the "Turing Test" to ask whether a machine can imitate human conversation.

1956

The term "Artificial Intelligence" is coined at the Dartmouth Conference.

1997

IBM's Deep Blue defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov.

2012

Deep learning breakthroughs dramatically improve image recognition accuracy.

2020s

Large language models bring conversational AI assistants into everyday use.
