Dream Holiday Guide

Singapore is calling. 🌴

A compact, no-fuss guide for first-time visitors — the best attractions, food and shopping Singapore has to offer, plus Kuala Lumpur lined up as your backup escape.

Choose your Singapore experience

01 — Popular Attractions

See the city's icons

From futuristic gardens to a half-lion statue — these are the sights every first-timer should hit.

🏙️ Attraction

Marina Bay Sands

Snap the iconic infinity pool skyline and catch the free light show — Singapore's most photographed landmark.

🌳 Attraction

Gardens by the Bay

Wander the futuristic Supertree Grove and glass domes bursting with rare plants — golden hour here is unmissable.

🦁 Attraction

Merlion Park

Grab the classic photo with Singapore's half-lion, half-fish icon, right on the Marina Bay waterfront.

🏝️ Attraction

Sentosa

Beaches, theme parks and a cable car ride, all on one island — a full day off the mainland grid.

02 — Food

Eat like a local

Hawker-stall classics that explain why Singaporeans talk about food more than the weather.

🍗 Local Food

Hainanese Chicken Rice

Silky poached chicken over fragrant rice — Singapore's national dish, and budget-friendly too.

🦀 Local Food

Chilli Crab

Whole crab in a sweet, spicy tomato-chilli sauce — best mopped up with a fried mantou bun.

🍲 Local Food

Laksa

Rich coconut-curry noodle soup with prawns and fish cake — comforting, spicy and deeply Peranakan.

🍢 Local Food

Satay

Charcoal-grilled skewers with peanut sauce — best enjoyed street-side as the sun goes down.

03 — Shopping

Shop the city

From air-conditioned malls to street markets — something for every budget.

🛍️ Shopping

Orchard Road

Singapore's famous shopping boulevard, packed with malls, flagship stores and air-conditioned relief.

🎪 Shopping

Bugis Street

A buzzing street market for cheap fashion, souvenirs and late-night snacking.

🏮 Shopping

Chinatown

Heritage shophouses, street stalls and temple-side bargains, all in one walkable district.

💧 Shopping

Jewel Changi Airport

Shop under the world's tallest indoor waterfall — even a layover feels like a destination.

Backup Destination

Plan B: Kuala Lumpur

Just an hour's flight from Singapore — here's why KL makes a solid alternative if plans change.

🗼 Attraction

Petronas Twin Towers

Twin steel giants with a skybridge view — KL's answer to Marina Bay Sands.

⛰️ Attraction

Batu Caves

Climb 272 rainbow-painted steps to limestone caves and a giant golden statue, just outside the city.

🍚 Local Food

Nasi Lemak

Coconut rice, sambal and crispy anchovies — Malaysia's favourite breakfast, sold on every corner.

🌃 Shopping

Bukit Bintang

KL's shopping and nightlife hub, from luxury malls to street-food alleys.
