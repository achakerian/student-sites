About this diary

Small recipes for hungry students

Banana Pancakes

Soft, sweet pancakes made with one banana and basic pantry ingredients.

Open recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1 tsp baking powder

Method

  1. Mash the banana.
  2. Mix in egg and milk.
  3. Add flour and baking powder.
  4. Cook small pancakes for 1–2 minutes per side.

Diary note: Looks fancy. Requires almost no talent.

Lunch10 min

Avocado Egg Toast

Crunchy toast, creamy avocado and a fried egg. Minimal effort, maximum brunch energy.

Open recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper
  • Chilli flakes, optional

Method

  1. Toast the bread.
  2. Mash avocado with salt and pepper.
  3. Fry the egg.
  4. Spread avocado on toast and add the egg.

Diary note: Café food without the café bill.

Lazy Tomato Pasta

A quick tomato pasta for the classic “I have no idea what to eat” evening.

Open recipe

Ingredients

  • 180 g pasta
  • 1 cup canned tomatoes
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper and dried herbs

Method

  1. Boil the pasta.
  2. Cook garlic in olive oil.
  3. Add tomatoes and seasonings, then simmer.
  4. Toss the pasta through the sauce.

Diary note: If you add cheese, suddenly it feels intentional.

Fridge-Cleanout Fried Rice

The best way to turn yesterday's rice and random vegetables into an actual meal.

Open recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cold cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1–2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp cooking oil

Method

  1. Scramble the eggs and set aside.
  2. Cook the vegetables.
  3. Add cold rice and stir until hot.
  4. Return egg, add soy sauce and mix.

Diary note: The fridge looked empty. Apparently it was lying.
