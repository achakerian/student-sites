MY DREAM HOLIDAY

Shanghai

A city where traditional Chinese culture meets a modern skyline. Shanghai is my dream destination because it looks exciting, beautiful and full of things to discover.

01 — WHY SHANGHAI?

A city I would love to discover.

I would love to visit Shanghai because it has a unique mix of old and new. I could walk through traditional gardens in the morning and see a modern city skyline at night. I also want to try local food and experience everyday life in one of China's most exciting cities.

02 — PLACES TO VISIT

Three places on my list

01

The Bund

I want to walk along the waterfront and enjoy the famous view of Shanghai's modern skyline across the Huangpu River.

02

Yu Garden

Yu Garden would be a chance to experience traditional Chinese architecture, peaceful gardens and local culture.

03

Shanghai Tower

I would love to see Shanghai from high above and enjoy the amazing view of the city from the observation area.

Explore Yu Garden

Start the day with traditional buildings, gardens and a relaxing walk.

13:00

Try Shanghai Food

Walk Along The Bund

Watch the city lights come on and enjoy the beautiful evening view.

See The Skyline

Finish the day by enjoying Shanghai's spectacular night skyline.
